# Code of conduct

TI-AAA is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, education, or employment status.

Contributors are expected to:

- communicate constructively and assume good intent
- give actionable technical feedback
- respect privacy, especially around applicant data and employment outcomes
- avoid discriminatory, demeaning, sexualized, threatening, or harassing behavior
- accept moderator decisions made to protect the community

Unacceptable conduct may be removed and repeated or severe violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer contact or GitHub's reporting tools. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards fairly.
